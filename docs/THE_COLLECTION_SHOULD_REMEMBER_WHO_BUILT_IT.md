# The collection should remember who built it

*Mask Born Order is growing from 210 traits to 1,000, and from 19 one-of-ones to 100. The community will help create what comes next.*

NFTs were supposed to bring creators and communities closer. Somewhere along the way, too many projects turned that promise into a conveyor belt.

Sell the dream. Dress AI-made work in the language of hand-drawn or generative art. Give the community a roadmap to defend, hope everyone flips, then move on when the attention does.

We want to build something harder to fake.

Mask Born Order already has its base character, generator, rarity model and 10,000-token structure. It currently contains 210 traits and 19 handcrafted 1/1s. Now we want to open part of that creative process and grow the collection to 1,000 traits and 100 1/1s.

Not by filling folders with random accessories.

By letting the community earn a place in the collection.

## Born from the same base, made by different hands

Members can enter the Mask Born studio and work directly on the real 32×32 character base.

They can create a complete 1/1 or design new Background, Eyes, Hats and Special traits. The editor keeps every trait on its own pixel layer, so a submission remains editable and can be tested against the existing collection.

Each creator gets two lifetime opportunities to submit a 1/1. They can also submit once in each supported trait category. The limits are intentional. We want people to spend time on their ideas instead of throwing endless variations at the gallery.

Published work enters community review. Voting stays open for 24 hours, and multi-trait submissions are judged one trait at a time. A brilliant Hat should not carry a weak Eye trait into the collection.

Votes matter, but they do not make the final decision. Popularity is useful information, not an art director. Every accepted piece still has to fit the Mask Born style, work with the existing traits and survive the actual generator pipeline.

## Acceptance means more than a gallery badge

When the team accepts a submission, it does not silently appear in the collection.

We export the exact pixel data, verify its hash, check compatibility, assign its final name, index and rarity, then add it to the canonical generator. The collection is regenerated and tested before that work becomes part of the official art.

That process is slower than running a vote and pressing "add."

Good. It should be.

A community-built collection still needs taste. Otherwise, "community-built" becomes an excuse for a folder full of disconnected artwork.

## If your 1/1 creates value, you should share in it

The plan for accepted 1/1 creators goes beyond attribution.

When an accepted 1/1 becomes part of the on-chain collection, its creator remains connected to that piece. If the 1/1 trades, the creator is intended to receive a defined portion of the trading fees generated by that artwork.

The system is already being structured around individual fee shares, marketplace trade events, creator accruals and payouts. That accounting matters. A creator should not contribute a piece, watch it become valuable and disappear into a credits page.

This revenue system is not live yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. The exact percentage, supported marketplaces and payout rules must be published before launch. Until then, accepted creators can be recorded as eligible without showing imaginary balances.

## Build the culture before selling it

We are not looking for a community that arrives on mint day, repeats the right slogans and leaves when the floor gets quiet.

We want artists, collectors and strange creative minds to leave something real inside Mask Born Order. Some will design a trait that appears across the collection. Others may create one of the 100 pieces that can never be rolled twice.

The goal is simple: stop treating "community" as another word for customers.

Give people the tools to contribute. Curate the results properly. Credit the hands behind the work. And when a community-made 1/1 produces value, keep its creator in the story.

**Born from the same base. Made by different hands.**

If you can make a 32×32 grid feel alive, enter the Order.
